# FlowPilot AI — 3-Minute Video Demo Storyboard

**Title**: FlowPilot AI — Autonomous Financial Operations Platform  
**Target Duration**: 3:00 Minutes  
**Voiceover Tone**: Professional, energetic, confident fintech founder.

---

### Segment 1: The Problem & Introduction (0:00 - 0:35)
- **Visual**: Screen recording opens on modern dark landing page with FlowPilot banner and emerald glow.
- **Voiceover**:
  > *"Every digital agency owner, freelancer, and merchant faces the same headache: chasing invoices, manually copying checkout links, and reconciling bank statements across disjointed tools. Traditional software gives you passive dashboards. Chatbots give you hallucinations. Meet FlowPilot AI: an autonomous financial operations platform powered by PayPal."*
- **Action**: Click "Explore Live Demo" and use the 1-Click Demo Login to enter the dashboard.

---

### Segment 2: Natural Language Invoice Creation & PayPal Sandbox (0:35 - 1:15)
- **Visual**: Switch to `/dashboard/agent`.
- **Voiceover**:
  > *"Let's see FlowPilot in action. I can simply type: 'Create a $500 invoice for John for website development and prepare a PayPal payment link.' Notice the visible execution timeline: FlowPilot understands the intent, queries our PostgreSQL database, creates the customer invoice, and calls PayPal's Orders API v2 in Sandbox."*
- **Action**: Click the suggested prompt. The 6-stage execution timeline animates in real-time. The verified PayPal Checkout URL appears. Click "Pay Now via PayPal" to display the official sandbox checkout screen.

---

### Segment 3: Real-Time Verification ("Did John Pay?") & Approvals Governance (1:15 - 1:55)
- **Visual**: Back in the AI Agent chat, type *"Did John pay?"*.
- **Voiceover**:
  > *"Because FlowPilot is grounded in real database records, it never invents numbers. When I ask 'Did John pay?', it checks the ledger and reports the invoice is pending payment. Next, let's look at risk governance. When I instruct FlowPilot to issue a refund or prepare overdue reminders, it detects a high-impact operation and displays an authorization card. In our Approvals Inbox, business owners have complete human-in-the-loop control."*
- **Action**: Show the high-risk approval card, then navigate to `/dashboard/approvals` and click "Authorize & Execute".

---

### Segment 4: AG Grid Payment Intelligence & Reconciliation (1:55 - 2:35)
- **Visual**: Navigate to `/dashboard/payments` (AG Grid Community) and `/dashboard/reconciliation`.
- **Voiceover**:
  > *"For financial visibility, FlowPilot features AG Grid Community for high-speed transaction filtering, multi-column sorting, and instant CSV exports. Under Reconciliation, our heuristic engine compares external PayPal captures with internal invoices. It flags discrepancies and orphaned transactions with explainable confidence scores, allowing one-click resolution."*
- **Action**: Filter columns in AG Grid, open the transaction drawer, switch to Reconciliation, and click "Resolve" on a discrepancy.

---
